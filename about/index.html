About Us

My Poker Gym is built by a team of poker fanatics looking for a new way to train poker and dominate low-stakes games.

We’ve tested the GTO-optimized platforms and studied range charts ad nauseam, but what we wanted was a simulator that felt like playing a real game and helped explain the thinking behind the optimal plays as well as forced thinking on how different players operate at the table and how to handle that variation. We use AI to assess thousands of hands and help you know the optimal decision to make with all different hands at all positions at the table (and factoring who else is sitting there with you).

TLDR: we wanted to build a poker trainer that functioned like how we want to play.

We read everything you send us. If you have an idea, find a spot that looks wrong or just want to talk poker, write to us at support@mypokergym.com. Our team is based in the US (feel free to email us to set up a game too :)

- Charlie and team

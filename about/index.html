We play the same games you do.

My Poker Gym is built by Charlie and a small team of amateur poker players. We wanted a better way to train for the games we actually play, so we made one.

None of us are pros. We mostly play low-stakes cash games, the kind of tables where you see limpers, calling stations, the occasional maniac and the regular who never seems to make a mistake. Like a lot of players, we wanted to stop guessing in those spots and start winning them.

Most training tools we tried were built for online grinders and high-stakes regulars. They were powerful, but they did not look much like the games we play. They rarely accounted for who was in the pot, and they often handed us an answer without showing why it was right.

So we built the table we wished we could practice at: real player tendencies, multiway pots like the ones you see live, and the math shown on every decision. My Poker Gym is for players like us who want to dominate the low stakes, one rep at a time.

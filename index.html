Reads you earn

Everyone sits down as a stranger. Their VPIPVPIP: voluntarily put money in potThe share of hands where a player chooses to put chips in before the flop, by calling or raising. Posting a blind doesn't count. Around 12 to 20% is tight, and 40% or more is very loose. and PFRPFR: preflop raiseThe share of hands a player raises before the flop. Read it next to VPIP: 50/5 means they play half their hands but almost never raise, the classic calling station. 22/18 means most of their hands come in with a raise. fill in hand by hand, just like the notes you'd keep at the casino.

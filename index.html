How "correct" is decided

Preflop answers come from charts modeled on solver ranges for 150-big-blind stacks, adjusted for each opponent's style. Postflop answers come from simulating your hand against each opponent's likely range, narrowed by everything they've done, plus solver-style rules for betting and sizing.

It's a strong training approximation built for live low-stakes games — not a full solver output.
